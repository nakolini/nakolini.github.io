Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Less or More is a mental arithmetic game. This policy describes, in plain language, what the app does with data. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect personal information, usage data, analytics, or identifiers. There is no account, no login, and no server we run.

What the app stores on your device

Two things, both local to your iPhone and both removed when you delete the app:

  • Your results — games played, scores, correct and wrong answers, per mode and per level. Kept in a file inside the app's own storage. The Clear button on the records screen erases it.
  • Your settings — chosen language, theme, vibration, and the last mode and level you played.

Duel results are not stored at all: they exist while the result screen is open and are gone when you leave it.

Network

The app contains no networking code. It never connects to the internet, and it works exactly the same in airplane mode.

Third-party SDKs

None. There is no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking code of any kind. The only third-party code is Apple's own frameworks.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not ask for photos, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or notifications.

Purchases and advertising

The app is free. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising.

Children

The game is suitable for children, and the levels are built around what children learn in school. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included, and contains nothing that leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear at this URL with a new effective date. There is no mechanism to notify you directly, because we do not have your contact details.
