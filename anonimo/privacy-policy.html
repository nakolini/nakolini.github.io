Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 19, 2026

Anonimo is a privacy tool. This policy describes, in plain language, what data the app processes and what leaves your device. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Anonimo does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or identifiers. There is no account. There is no login. There is no server we run.

How your videos and photos are processed

When you open a video or photo in Anonimo, the file is read from your Photos library and processed entirely on your iPhone. Face detection runs on-device using Apple's Vision framework and a local ArcFace model bundled with the app. The original file and the processed output stay in your device's storage.

The app never uploads media, embeddings, or metadata to a server. The app does not request or require an internet connection to function.

Third-party SDKs

Anonimo does not include any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party code is Apple's own frameworks (Vision, Core Image, AVFoundation, StoreKit).

In-App Purchases

Purchases of Anonimo Pro (yearly subscription or lifetime) are processed by Apple's App Store. Your payment information is handled entirely by Apple and never reaches us. We receive an anonymous receipt from Apple confirming your entitlement; we do not receive your name, email, or payment details.

Permissions

  • Photos access — required so you can pick a video or photo to process, and so the processed result can be saved back to Photos. Anonimo only reads items you explicitly select via the Photos picker.

Children

Anonimo is not specifically directed at children under 13, but it is safe for all ages. The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, the updated version will appear at this URL with a new effective date. There is no mechanism to notify you directly because we do not have your contact information.
